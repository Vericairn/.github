# Contributing to Vericairn

This is the contributing guide for every Vericairn repository that has none of its own.

## Issues are welcome

Bug reports and feature requests help. Open an issue in the repository it concerns and choose the form that fits.
Search the open and closed issues first, and check the known limitations in the documentation set of the release
you run: many limits are stated there.

A good bug report:

- says what happened, with the exact message or refusal, and what you expected instead;
- gives the steps to reproduce it, from a fresh estate where you can;
- includes the output of `vericairn version`, your platform, and whether you used Workbench or the command line;
- leaves out secrets, keys, tokens and anything from your estate you would not publish.

A good feature request starts from the outcome you want and the situation you need it in, rather than from a
particular design, and names the alternatives you considered.

Keep each issue to one problem or one request.

## Outside pull requests are not accepted yet

The terms on which Vericairn accepts outside contributions are decided at milestone M4. Until then, pull requests
from outside are not accepted. Open an issue instead, and describe the problem or the change you have in mind.

Vericairn Pro, whose source is published for review, takes no outside contributions at all, and neither will
Vericairn's first-party extensions.

## Security

Never report a vulnerability in a public issue or pull request. Report it privately, as the
[security policy](SECURITY.md) describes.

## Conduct

Everyone who takes part is expected to follow the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
